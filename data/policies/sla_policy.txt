[SLA & PRIORITY ESCALATION POLICY]
- Priority 1 (P1): Triggered when customer_tier is "enterprise" OR the ticket contains critical keywords like "outage", "system down", "security breach", or "data leak". Action: Set escalate=true and prioritize immediate routing to the relevant specialist queue.
- Priority 2 (P2): High urgency issues, issues from "pro" tier customers, or service disputes (e.g., cannot log in, billing dispute, UI blocking bugs, parcel lost). Action: Set escalate=false.
- Priority 3 (P3): Normal inquiries or standard system behavior questions from "free" tier customers. Action: Set escalate=false.
- Priority 4 (P4): General feedback, compliments, or minor feature requests. Action: Set escalate=false.
